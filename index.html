About MOTEQAR

Driven by Creativity.
Powered by Technology.

MOTEQAR is a technology company focused on transforming ideas into meaningful digital solutions. We design, build, and scale software, applications, and digital products that help businesses and people move forward.

Our approach is simple: understand the real problem, design with people in mind, and engineer solutions that last. Beyond client work, we are building our own ecosystem of digital products for the long term.

Inspired by "Motékar"

A Sundanese word for creativity, resourcefulness, and the ability to find innovative solutions. It is the spirit behind everything we build.

Technology should not simply solve today's problems. It should create opportunities for tomorrow.

Vision

To become a trusted technology company that creates meaningful innovations and contributes to a better digital future.

Mission

  1. Develop innovative and impactful digital solutions.
  2. Empower businesses through technology.
  3. Build accessible and useful digital products.
  4. Encourage creativity and continuous innovation.
  5. Create sustainable technology ecosystems.
